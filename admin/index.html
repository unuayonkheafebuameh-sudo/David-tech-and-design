Update your website in 4 steps

  1. Add or edit projects, services and contact details here.
  2. Open Export to Website and tap Copy code.
  3. In index.html, replace everything from the line EDIT YOUR DETAILS HERE to the closing /* ===== */ line.
  4. Upload the updated index.html (and any downloaded images in an images folder) to your host.
